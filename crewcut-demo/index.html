Instant lawn prices, in every Crewcut region.

A customer types their address and sees a price in about a minute, instead of waiting for a quote. The job goes straight to the local crew, with the lawn already measured.

  1. Type the addressAnywhere in NZ. It finds the nearest Crewcut region by drive time.
  2. We measure itThe section, the lawn and the roadside berm, from the property itself. Cross-lease homes are picked up.
  3. Price and jobThe customer sees a price range. The crew gets the measured shapes to check before the first mow.

What the crew gets. Each quote comes with the measured shapes on the photo, so the crew can check it before the first mow, plus the section and house size, cross-lease, drive time from their base, directions and a printable job sheet.
