Blocked drain?

Signs your drain is blocked

Spot any of these? Call Phil before it backs up.

  • Sinks or showers draining slowly
  • Gurgling from the toilet or plugholes
  • A bad smell from drains, inside or out
  • Toilet water rising when you flush
  • Gully trap overflowing outside
  • More than one drain blocked at once

About

Phil and A1 Drain Unblockers

A1 Drain Unblockers is Phillip Mercer's business, based in Levin. Phil clears blocked drains, toilets and sewer pipes for homes and businesses across Horowhenua, from Levin and Foxton to Shannon and Ōtaki.

Locals call him because he turns up quickly, often within the hour, charges a fair price and explains why the drain blocked so it doesn't happen again.

Questions

Common questions

Do you have to dig up my lawn or driveway?

No. Phil clears the blockage from inside the pipe with his drain machine. No digging required.

Do you work weekends and public holidays?

Yes. Customers have had Phil out on a Sunday, over Easter weekend and the day after Boxing Day.

What causes a blocked drain?

Most often tree roots growing into the sewer pipe, or grease and fat building up in the main drain. Phil will tell you what he found.

Do you do businesses and industrial sites?

Yes. A1 Drain Unblockers is domestic and industrial.

What areas do you cover?

Levin, Foxton, Shannon, Ōtaki and the wider Horowhenua.
