Our work

Our projects

A selection of our recent and past work, from new builds to character renovations. Swipe through each project, including before and during shots.

Renovation and extension · 2023

A larger, modern home in Mangorei

Our clients wanted to stay central while creating a larger, modern home, and we are proud of how seamlessly old meets new. A detailed renovation with a dug-out three-bay garage and extensive retaining. Inside: matte black finishes, gunmetal hardware and custom spaces, opening onto a spacious kwila deck with a fully covered louvre system for year-round entertaining.

  • Dug-out three-bay garage
  • Extensive retaining
  • Kwila deck with covered louvres
Garage extension · 2024

A guest retreat above the garage

We added a self-contained guest area above an existing single-storey garage, creating a versatile family and rental retreat. Carefully designed to blend in, it looks right at home next to the existing villa. Inside, a considered interior with plenty of fine details: timber joinery, brass tapware and a built-in window seat.

  • Single storey to two
  • Self-contained guest area
  • Designed to match the villa
Character home renovation · 2020

A 1926 character home, modernised

Built in 1926, this character home had plenty of charm but needed a better layout and modern features. By changing internal walls and removing the wood fire we created a new open-plan kitchen, dining and lounge, plus a new laundry and toilet. Skylights and new wooden joinery complete a warm, welcoming home that blends old and new.

  • New open-plan kitchen
  • New laundry and toilet
  • Skylights and wooden joinery
Bach renovation · 2024

Two units made one at Lake Rotoiti

With a rapidly growing extended family, this holiday home needed more space. After the duplex next door was bought, we opened up the two units to create one cohesive living area, finished just in time for the family to enjoy over summer.

  • Two units joined into one
  • Open-plan living
  • Finished for summer
Bungalow extension · 2024

More room for a growing family in Fitzroy

With a growing family in mind, we added a new master bedroom, walk-in wardrobe and internal double garage to this Fitzroy bungalow by using the unused front lawn, and renovated the existing living room. Cedar features and a double gable roofline create a statement entrance that suits the coastal surrounds.

  • Master bedroom and walk-in wardrobe
  • Internal double garage
  • Cedar and double gable entrance
Outdoor area · 2025

A backyard built for the whole family

A new backyard designed with the whole family in mind: a built-in slide, in-ground trampoline, spa and plenty of flat lawn for football practice. A fun, low-maintenance setup that gets used all year round.

  • Built-in slide
  • In-ground trampoline and spa
  • Low-maintenance
New build · 2017

A new home built in stages

One of our older builds: this home was added onto in stages as the budget allowed. We completed the main house, with one of our first apprentices finishing the job. Light-filled, modern living surrounded by tropical greenery.

  • Built in stages
  • Light-filled living
  • Indoor-outdoor flow
New build · 2017

A low-maintenance home for downsizing

Built for a client moving from the farm into town. The brief: comfortable, accessible and low-maintenance, with a sunny nook for the armchair and room for the roses.

  • Accessible layout
  • Low-maintenance
  • Sunny living spaces

About

About Luke Nolly Building

Luke Nolly Building is a New Plymouth building company, established in 2014 and led by Luke Nolly.

We build new homes and take on renovations, extensions and outdoor areas, from a 1926 character home that needed a modern layout to a garage with a guest retreat above it, and even a bach on Lake Rotoiti. Luke is involved in every project from planning to handover.

A New Plymouth villa renovation we were main builder on was a finalist in Your Home and Garden's Reno of the Year (2019).

We are proud to train apprentices. In 2026 one of ours placed second at the regional Apprentice of the Year awards.

Questions

Common questions

What kind of work do you take on?

New homes, renovations, extensions, garage extensions, decks and outdoor areas.

Do you renovate older homes?

Yes. We have renovated character homes, including one built in 1926, opening up the layout while keeping its charm.

Can a new build be done in stages?

Yes. We have built homes in stages as the budget allowed.

What areas do you cover?

New Plymouth and the wider Taranaki region.
