About

About TD Building

TD Building is owned and operated by Tommy Death, a Licensed Building Practitioner with over 16 years in the trade. Born and raised in Eltham, Tommy has a strong connection to Taranaki and now works from New Plymouth.

We specialise in renovations, new builds, landscaping and roofing, from a single bathroom to a full house transformation, a new roof on a bach or a seven-bay farm shed. TD Building is a member of New Zealand Certified Builders.

Our clients mention clear communication, work finished on time and to budget, fair and transparent pricing with no hidden costs, and a tidy site when we leave.

Questions

Common questions

Are you a licensed builder?

Yes. Tommy is a Licensed Building Practitioner and TD Building is a member of New Zealand Certified Builders.

What kind of work do you take on?

Bathroom, kitchen, laundry and whole-home renovations, new builds and sheds, full roof replacements, and decks, stairs, retaining walls and planter boxes.

Do you fix leaking homes?

Yes. We find and fix leaks, and can gut and rebuild the affected rooms into a warm, modern space.

Do you work on farms and baches?

Yes. Our projects include farm sheds and new roofs on baches as well as family homes.

What areas do you cover?

New Plymouth and the wider Taranaki region.
