About

About K+M Projects

K+M Projects is owned and operated by Kyle and Mitch, two qualified Licensed Building Practitioners local to Christchurch. We are a member of New Zealand Certified Builders.

Between us we have more than 25 years in the construction industry, from small decks and fences to residential architectural builds and full large-scale commercial management. We work across Christchurch, Selwyn and Ashburton.

Our clients mention clear communication, turning up when we say we will, clear quotes, and a clean and tidy worksite.

Questions

Common questions

Are you licensed builders?

Yes. Kyle and Mitch are both qualified Licensed Building Practitioners, and K+M Projects is a member of New Zealand Certified Builders.

What kind of work do you take on?

Decks, pergolas and fences, renovations and alterations, new homes and architectural builds, re-roofing, and light commercial work.

Can you help with insurance repairs?

Yes. We carry out repairs and can work with your insurer to take the hassle off you.

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Our projects include commercial re-roofs and light commercial builds, and we offer project management.

What areas do you cover?

Christchurch, Selwyn (including Rolleston) and Ashburton.
